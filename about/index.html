A client-side suite for small-arms external ballistics. Everything runs in your browser — no data leaves the device. It works offline once loaded, and installs as an app on desktop and mobile.

Open the app →

Tools

Trajectory

Bullets don't fly straight. Here's exactly how far off — drop, windage, velocity, time of flight, at any distance.

Hit Probability

Hit probability against a target, accounting for wind, range-estimation and dispersion uncertainty.

Range Solver

Refined "high speed low drag" ballistic solver for range use. No unnecessary information.

BC Tools

Calculate BC from known data, convert between different models, etc.

Cd–Mach Curve

Build a bullet's own drag curve from a velocity table.

Rifle Precision Calculator

Measure rifle precision using images of targets with impacts.

Privacy

We do not collect any data. Nothing is ever transmitted to the server.
